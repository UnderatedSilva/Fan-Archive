THE ARTIST

An artist who turned concerts into experiences
- instead of performances

Born on 22 December 1994 in Kandy, Sri Lanka, Dhanith Sri is a singer, composer and songwriter widely regarded as one of the most popular and influential artists in Sri Lanka today. Educated at Vidyartha College and Kingswood College in Kandy, he went on to graduate with a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the University of Peradeniya — before choosing music over engineering and changing the Sri Lankan music landscape forever.

He started playing guitar after his A/Ls, self-taught through friends and the internet. He writes his lyrics and melodies simultaneously — they arrive together, never one before the other. A singer, painter, dancer and storyteller, Dhanith is a complete artist in every sense of the word. He entered the mainstream with his 2018 fusion breakthrough Pandama — and never looked back.

THE PROCESS

Art born from within.

“When a song is about to be born, I have a certain structure in my mind. Both the lyrics and the melody come to me together — I don't compose lyrics to a melody, and I don't create melodies to previously written lyrics. I just let it flow.”

— Dhanith Sri

“Ideas for songs envelop my mind at the most unexpected times. I feed the ideas and let them grow slowly — let them bloom beautifully at the moment they deserve to be seen and heard.”

THE RAP CHAPTER

Raplanthaya

In 2023 Dhanith Sri surprised the entire Sri Lankan music scene by dropping a full rap album — Raplanthaya. A bold genre pivot that proved his range had no ceiling. The album spawned two award-winning music videos and cemented his status as one of the most versatile artists in the country.

LIVE IN CONCERT

Alokawarsha

The Tale of Limitless Minds

Dhanith Sri's landmark solo concert series — Alokawarsha: The Tale of Limitless Minds — stands as a groundbreaking musical experience in Sri Lanka. A night that proved there are no limits to what Sri Lankan music can achieve on a live stage.

Live recordings from Alokawarsha 2025 featuring Pandama, Sandaganawa, Alokawarsha, and Niwalanna Ma with Senanga Dissanayake are available on streaming.
